# 06-orbitals

ZINDO/S orbitals and CIS states at the trans and cis minima, ORCA 6.1.1, with MO coefficients printed and
cube files for MOs 29–38. Geometries: trans = point 180° of the torsion envelope (same as `01-opt/trans.xyz`
within the scan convergence), cis = `cis_min.xyz` (copy of `01-opt/cis.xyz`, CNNC 5.8°).
`cis.*` is the 0° point of the torsion envelope (CNNC constrained to 0°, 0.8 kJ/mol above the cis minimum),
kept for comparison.

```
/usr/local/bin/orca_6_1_1/orca trans.inp > trans.out
python ../scripts/mo_character.py trans.out trans.xyz
grep -A4 "STATE  1:" trans.out
```

`mo_character.py` prints, for MOs 29–38, the fraction of c² on the N atoms and the fraction of p functions
along the normal of the C2–N0=N1 plane (π). ZINDO uses an orthogonal basis, so c² sums to 1.
For cis the phenyl rings are twisted out of the CNNC plane, so ring orbitals show π ≈ 0.3.
